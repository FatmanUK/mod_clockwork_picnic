## Pattern `05` — Clockwork Games A

Patterns `05–06` form the **Clockwork Games** pair. They retain the accepted three-voice piano model but make the arrangement more playful through steady accompaniment, repeated harmonic shapes, and delayed PingBell answers.

Channel duties are:

* **CH1:** piano top voice
* **CH2:** piano inner voice, temporarily yielding to PingBells
* **CH3:** consolidated percussion, becoming a fourth piano voice at the final cadence
* **CH4:** piano lowest voice

SoftBass remains out for now. Introducing it here would cost one of the piano voices immediately after the fuller piano texture finally passed audition, which would be an impressively efficient way to undo our own progress.

The harmonic route is approximately:

```text
C → G → Am → Em → F → Dm → G
```

The PingBells arrive at rows `10`, `26`, and `42`, two rows after the preceding piano attacks. That makes them answers rather than chord members. CH2 then returns to piano six rows later, giving each bell enough time to ring without colonising the pattern.

The ending is deliberately dominant:

```text
CH4  G-3
CH2  B-3
CH1  D-4
```

That complete G-major triad carries directly into Pattern `06`.

---

The melody and piano voicing can remain unchanged. The correction is to move each bell onto the next four-row beat, turning it into a clear **beat-four pickup** before the next harmonic phrase.

For both patterns:

* remove the bell-only rows `10`, `26`, and `42`;
* place those bells on rows `12`, `28`, and `44`;
* leave every other event unchanged.
