# Pattern `04` — Main Theme Development B

This one answers `03`, pulls us firmly back toward C, and introduces **PingBells only three times**. The hats simply yield those moments rather than forcing a fifth imaginary channel into existence.

The final eight rows deliberately echo the small turnaround language already established in `01` and `02`:

```text
E-4 → G-4 → C-4 → B-3 → C-4
```

That should make the whole four-pattern main-theme block feel related rather than sequentially generated.

The three PingBells at rows `24`, `40`, and `56` act as little reflected glints on cadential points. They are answers to the piano, not another melody.

For audition, I’d test the pair exactly as requested:

```text
03, 04
```

Then, if they pass together, test the complete thematic span:

```text
01, 02, 03, 04
```

The important question for `03–04` is whether they feel like **development of the pianist established in `01–02`**, while the filter opening and sparse bells provide enough new colour to keep four consecutive patterns from feeling static.

For the joint audition, use:


The main things to judge are whether CH1 and CH2 read as a coordinated right hand, whether CH4 gives the piano enough lower-body movement, whether the rolled chords sound natural rather than gimmicky, and whether losing a few drum hits at the cadences makes those arrivals feel played rather than sequenced.
