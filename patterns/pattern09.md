This develops the first solo pattern rather than merely repeating it:

```text
A-3 → C-4 → E-4 → G-4
A-4 → G-4 → F-4 → E-4
B-3 → A-3
```

`A-4` at row `32` is the solo’s high point. From there, the line makes a continuous descent toward the final `A-3`.

The closing harmony is:

```text
D minor → E major / E7 → A minor
```

So the question posed by Pattern `08` receives an actual tonal answer rather than a vague nod from across the room.
